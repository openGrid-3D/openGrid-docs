Gridfinity is a popular modular storage system created by Zack Freedman that uses a 42mm grid pattern. openGrid is compatible with Gridfinity through a simple mathematical relationship.

## Grid compatibility

The grids align at an 84mm common interval:

- **2 Gridfinity units** = 84mm (2 × 42mm)
- **3 openGrid units** = 84mm (3 × 28mm)

Gridfinity accessories sized in even multiples (2×2, 4×2, etc.) will align with openGrid's grid positions. Most accessories work directly without modification, though some may need minor edge clearance trimming.

## Shelves

Dedicated Gridfinity shelf systems provide the cleanest integration:

- [Gridfinity shelves on printables](https://www.printables.com/model/1214460-opengrid-gridfinity-shelves)
- [Gridfinity shelves on makerworld](https://makerworld.com/en/models/1169053-opengrid-gridfinity-shelves)
- [Infinite Gridfinity shelf on makerworld](https://makerworld.com/en/models/1469935-infinite-gridfinity-shelf-for-opengrid-very-strong) — stronger construction for heavy loads

## Using Multiconnect

Multiconnect snaps also provide access to the Gridfinity ecosystem. See the [Multiconnect guide](./multiconnect/) for details.

## Installation tips

- Use directional or lock snaps for shelves holding heavier loads
- Distribute weight across multiple grid points rather than concentrating it
- Test fit accessories before printing large quantities

## Community resources

The Gridfinity ecosystem has thousands of community-designed accessories. Search for `opengrid gridfinity` on Printables or MakerWorld.
